About Me

I help ecommerce brands and online businesses build and automate systems that scale. Many businesses struggle with disconnected platforms, manual processes, and systems that don’t grow with them. I specialize in turning those setups into clean, reliable workflows that improve user experience, reduce friction, and save time.

I combine strong technical skills with a customer-first mindset shaped by years of client-facing experience. This allows me to translate business goals into practical, goal-driven solutions not just code for code’s sake.

When needed, I build custom logic and integrations using Python, Laravel, JavaScript, React, PHP, and MySQL to make platforms work together seamlessly.

Whether it’s optimizing a Shopify store, streamlining operations in GoHighLevel, or improving a Thinkific training system, my goal is always the same: build systems that are efficient, scalable, and easy to maintain.
